# This is a Tip Calculator
This is a very simple tip calculator app made using SwiftUI.
The app lets you type in a bill amount, choose a tip percentage, select how many people are splitting the bill up, and then shows you the tip and total amount.

## What the app does
- You enter the bill amount
- You choose a tip percentage
- You can also choose how many people are splitting the bill (up to 20 people) 
- The app will calculate the tip
- The app then shows the total amount
- You can scroll if everything does not fit on the screen

## Dark Mode
My phone is in Dark Mode (At least it is for me)
Because of that, I used a white background with black text for the results so they are easy to read.
